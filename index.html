Focus Buddy

A Reachy Mini that notices when you touch your face — and says something.

Runs on-robot or on your laptop Fully offline by default Nothing stored

What it does

It watches for the small habits that break your focus and mentions them out loud, at most once a minute. Once an hour it tells you how the day is going. There is no score and no dashboard — the tally resets at midnight and nothing is uploaded.

🤦 Face touching A hand resting on your cheek, chin or forehead.
💅 Nail biting Fingertips at or in your mouth.
📱 Phone drift Phone in hand, eyes on it instead of the screen. Cloud backend only.

Try it

No robot required — it runs against your laptop webcam.

pip install -e .
cp .env.example .env      # add your OPENAI_API_KEY
focus-buddy --desktop

To run with no API key and no image ever leaving the machine, build the landmark sidecar once with focus-buddy-setup-sidecar, then start it with focus-buddy --desktop --backend edge.

How it works

Camera frame → Geometry or model → Observation → Nudge → Speech

Every vision backend returns the same typed Observation, so swapping a cloud model for a local one changes nothing downstream. Spoken nudges come from a fixed set of phrasings by default; if you let a language model rewrite them, whatever it produces has to pass validation first — right length, actually about the habit it saw, and not contradicting the frame.

Why geometry, not a vision model

"Is a hand touching your face" is a distance question, and small local vision models answer it badly. Measured on labelled frames from a Reachy Mini camera — half with a hand at the face, half without — every 2–3B checkpoint scored at chance by answering yes to all of them:

BackendFace-touch accuracyPer frame
SmolVLM2-2.2B-4bit8/16 (chance)13.3 s
Qwen2-VL-2B-4bit8/16 (chance)4.5 s
Qwen2.5-VL-3B-4bit8/16 (chance)3.1 s
landmark geometry24/240.05 s

The same frames separate cleanly by distance: 0.000–0.015 face widths while touching, 0.528–2.102 while not. Thresholds are ratios of the face width, so they do not care how close you sit or what resolution the camera runs at. Phone detection is the one thing geometry cannot do — a phone is object recognition, not a distance — so it is answered by the cloud backend or not at all.
